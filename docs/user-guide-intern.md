# Intern User Guide & Manual

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-04  
**Audience:** Enrolled Interns  
**Portal Version:** 4.0.0

---

## 1. First-Time Sign In & Password Setup

When you are registered for an internship, you receive an **Enrollment Email** containing your unique **Intern Code** (for example: `UTS-2026-0042`).

### Step 1: Initial Login
1. Navigate to the portal login screen at `/login`.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. In the password field, enter your assigned **Intern Code**.
4. Click **Sign In**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   UPTOSKILL PORTAL                     │
│                                                        │
│  Email:        [ intern.student@example.com          ] │
│  Password:     [ UTS-2026-0042                       ] │
│                                                        │
│                     [ Sign In ]                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 2: Establish Your Permanent Password
For account security, you must set a private password before requesting certificates:
1. Click **Settings** in the left navigation sidebar.
2. Under **Account Security**, enter your current secret (your Intern Code).
3. Enter your **New Password** (minimum 8 characters, must include at least one letter and one number).
4. Click **Save Password**.
5. Once set, your Intern Code is deactivated as a password—future logins will require your new private password.

## 2. Profile Management

You can update your personal phone number directly:
1. In the **Settings** tab, locate **Contact Information**.
2. Update your **Mobile Number** (10 to 15 digits).
3. Click **Update Profile**.
4. *Note: Full Name, Domain, and Internship Dates are locked by administrative records. Contact your Team Leader if corrections are required.*

---

## 3. Submitting a Certificate Request

Once you have completed your internship milestones or require official documentation:
1. From the **Dashboard**, click the **Request Certificate** button in the top action bar.
2. Select the required certificate type:
   - **Internship Completion Certificate**: Awarded upon successful capstone submission and final evaluation.
   - **Offer Letter**: Formal confirmation of your internship position and dates.
   - **Bonafide Certificate**: Used for university and academic verification.
   - **On-the-Job Training (OJT) Certificate**: Formal certificate detailing practical tasks.
   - **Experience Certificate**: Outlines roles, technologies, and hours served.
3. Provide an optional message or note for your Team Leader.
4. Click **Submit Request**.
5. *Note: Only one pending request per certificate type is permitted at a time.*

---

## 4. Tracking Request Status

Your **Certificates** dashboard tracks your submissions across each review stage:

| Status Badge | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pending** | Request received; queued for your assigned Team Leader to review. |
| **Forwarded** | Verified by your Team Leader; forwarded to Administration for drafting. |
| **Processing** | In administrative visual review, drafting, or PDF generation. |
| **Completed** | Certificate approved, finalized, cryptographically stamped, and ready for download. |
| **Rejected** | The request was returned by your TL or Admin with specific feedback notes. |

---

## 5. Downloading Your Finalized Certificate

1. When a certificate moves to **Completed**, you receive an email alert and an in-app notification in your bell icon.
2. Click **Download PDF** beside the certificate in your dashboard.
3. The downloaded PDF includes your official digital verification QR code. Anyone scanning this code with a mobile camera can instantly verify its authenticity on the portal.

---

## 6. Using the AI Support Assistant (Chatbot)

Need quick answers about deadlines, formatting, or certificate issuance?
1. Look at the bottom-right corner of your dashboard and click the floating **Need Help?** button.
2. A chat panel will slide up. Type your question (e.g. *"When will my certificate be ready?"* or *"How do I change my phone number?"*).
3. The assistant will reference UPTOSKILL knowledge base documentation to assist you in real time.
4. You can press `Enter` to send or `Escape` to close the widget.
5. If you have an inquiry that the chatbot cannot resolve, email `support@uptoskill.com`.

---

## 7. Account Archival Policy

- Following your scheduled internship end date, your account enters a **30-day Grace Period**.
- 7 days prior to archival, an email alert will remind you to download any remaining certificates.
- After the grace period, your account status is set to **Archived** and login access is closed.
- If you need your account restored, contact `support@uptoskill.com` before the 90-day privacy purge deadline.
