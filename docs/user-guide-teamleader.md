# Team Leader User Guide

**Last Updated:** 2026-11-04  
**Audience:** Team Leaders & Mentors  
**Portal Version:** 4.0.0

---

## 1. Team Leader Overview

As a Team Leader, you supervise cohorts of assigned interns, evaluate their certificate requests, and verify completion before forwarding to the Admin department.

### Scoping & Privacy Enforcement
- You have access **only** to the interns assigned to your team leader profile.
- You cannot inspect, approve, or reject certificate requests belonging to other Team Leaders.

---

## 2. Reviewing Certificate Requests

1. Log in to your account at `/login`.
2. The default view is the **Pending Reviews** queue.
3. Each pending card displays:
   - Intern's Full Name & Code
   - Certificate Type Requested
   - Intern's Domain and Completion Progress (%)
   - Submission Timestamp

### Forwarding a Request to Admin
1. Inspect the intern's work and confirm completion criteria are satisfied.
2. Click **Forward to Admin**.
3. The request status updates to `forwarded`, the intern receives a notification, and the request enters the Administration processing queue.

### Rejecting a Request
1. If the intern has not met project requirements:
2. Click **Reject**.
3. A modal opens prompting for **Rejection Reason** (mandatory).
4. Provide constructive feedback (e.g., *"Capstone documentation missing section 4; please revise and resubmit"*).
5. Click **Confirm Rejection**.
6. The intern is immediately notified with your feedback notes.

---

## 3. Monitoring Assigned Interns

1. Click **My Interns** in the sidebar.
2. Use the domain filter, status pills (`ongoing`, `completed`, `upcoming`), and search bar to locate specific team members.
3. Click any intern card to view their complete contact details, progress bar, start/end dates, and performance remarks.
4. You can edit performance remarks or project details by clicking **Edit Intern Details**.

---

## 4. Upcoming Completions Dashboard

- The **Upcoming Completions** widget highlights all interns finishing within the next 7 calendar days.
- Use this to prepare final reviews and ensure project evaluations are completed before the scheduled end date.

---

## 5. Notification Bell
- Located at the top right of your navigation header.
- A live badge counter displays new intern submissions in real time.
- Clicking any notification marks it as read and redirects you to the relevant review queue.
